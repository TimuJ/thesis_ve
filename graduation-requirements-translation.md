# Regulations on Innovation Achievements for Academic Degree Graduate Students Applying for Degrees

**Disciplines**: Computer Science and Technology, Software Engineering, Artificial Intelligence (Interdisciplinary)

Based on the "Implementation Measures for Graduate Degree Application at Zhejiang University (Trial)" (Zhejiang University Graduate Research [2020] No. 45), the "Regulations of the Faculty of Information Technology on Innovation Achievements for Academic Degree Graduate Students Applying for Degrees," and other relevant documents, combined with the characteristics of Computer Science and Technology, Software Engineering, and Artificial Intelligence interdisciplinary programs, these regulations are hereby formulated.

---

## Article 1

The innovation achievements used by graduate students to apply for Master's or Doctoral degrees shall be independently completed by the degree applicant during their degree program and presented in full in the form of a degree thesis. **The degree thesis is the primary basis for degree evaluation.**

## Article 2

Graduate students may present their innovation achievements in the form of academic journal papers, academic conference papers, monographs, patents, etc. Such innovation achievements may serve as **important references for evaluating the quality of the degree thesis**. Innovation achievements must be independently completed under the guidance of the supervisor (or supervisory committee), be related to the degree thesis, and list Zhejiang University as the first affiliated institution.

**The types of achievements are as follows:**

1. Published or accepted Category II or above papers recognized by the Discipline Degree Evaluation Committee (see Appendix);
2. Invention patent that has entered substantive examination;
3. Highest award in competitions recognized by the Discipline Degree Evaluation Committee (see Appendix);
4. Officially released national/international standard technical proposals;
5. Published academic works (including textbooks), as one of the authors of an academic work (including textbooks), with a minimum of 50,000 characters of writing completed;
6. National Science and Technology Award Second Prize or above (as a contributor) or Provincial/Ministerial First Prize (ranked in top 5), Second Prize (ranked in top 3). (For Master's students: Provincial/Ministerial level or above science and technology award, with no ranking requirement.)

## Article 3 — Doctoral Degree Requirements

Doctoral students applying for a degree should, in principle, meet one of the following conditions:

1. All doctoral thesis reviews receive an overall grade of "A (Excellent)" or "B (Good)," with no more than one "B (Good)." This method may be used to apply for a degree no more than once.
2. Obtain 3 or more innovation achievements, of which at least 1 must be a published or accepted Category I paper. The doctoral student, as paper author, patent inventor, or competition award winner, may be ranked first or second; if ranked second, the first-ranked person must be a member of the supervisory committee. As a contributor to an adopted national/international standard technical proposal, the student must be ranked first among students in the discipline.

## Article 4 — Master's Degree Requirements

Master's students applying for a degree should, in principle, meet one of the following conditions:

1. All Master's thesis reviews receive an overall grade of "A (Excellent)" or "B (Good)," with no more than one "B (Good)." This method may be used to apply for a degree no more than once.
2. **Obtain 1 or more innovation achievements.** The Master's student, as paper author or patent inventor, may be ranked first or second; if ranked second, the first-ranked person must be a member of the supervisory committee. As a competition award winner or contributor to an adopted national/international standard technical proposal, the student may be ranked in the top two among students (including second place).

## Article 5

Research achievements in interdisciplinary fields may be substituted with the recognition standards of the relevant cross-disciplinary fields.

## Article 6

For graduate students involved in classified/confidential projects, the requirements for thesis defense application may follow the university's relevant regulations.

## Article 7

For matters not covered herein, the "Implementation Measures for Graduate Degree Application at Zhejiang University (Trial)" (Zhejiang University Graduate Research [2020] No. 45) shall prevail.

## Article 8

These regulations take effect starting with graduate students enrolled from September 2021. Students enrolled before this date may, at the decision of their supervisor (or supervisory committee), follow the new regulations or continue under the previous regulations. These regulations are interpreted by the Discipline Degree Evaluation Committee.

**Computer Science Discipline Degree Evaluation Committee**
**December 20, 2021**

## Appendix

### I. Category I Papers

1. Category A papers from the "CCF (China Computer Federation) Recommended List of International Academic Conferences and Journals";
2. Category A papers from the "Tsinghua University New Edition Recommended List of Academic Conferences and Journals for Computer Science";
3. Papers in equally ranked high-level journals as recognized by the Discipline Degree Evaluation Committee.

### II. Category II Papers

1. Category B and C papers from the "CCF Recommended List of International Academic Conferences and Journals";
2. Category B papers from the "Tsinghua University New Edition Recommended List of Academic Conferences and Journals for Computer Science";
3. Papers in journals recognized by the Discipline Degree Evaluation Committee, including: *Journal of Software*, *Chinese Journal of Computers*, *Journal of Computer Research and Development*, *Journal of Computer-Aided Design and Computer Graphics*, *Acta Electronica Sinica*, *Acta Automatica Sinica*, *Journal of Zhejiang University*, *Visual Informatics*, *Virtual Reality & Intelligent Hardware*, *Blockchain: Research and Applications*, among others.

### III. Competition Categories

1. China "Internet+" College Students Innovation and Entrepreneurship Competition (organized by the Ministry of Education together with government agencies and universities);
2. China Graduate AI Innovation Competition (guided by the Degree and Graduate Education Development Center of the Ministry of Education, organized by the China Association for Science and Technology Youth Science and Technology Center);
3. Other competitions of equivalent level as recognized by the Discipline Degree Evaluation Committee.
