\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:exp:setup}

\paragraph{Base videos.} Five long-form base videos (identified as 7WHI, BrRLK, KZ,
hhsz, and mJog throughout), $80$--$208$ seconds each, $2{,}412$--$5{,}000$ frames,
$22{,}412$ frames in total, degraded by the DOVE pipeline
(Section~\ref{sec:found:dove}) to $320\times180$ and super-resolved to
$1280\times720$.

\paragraph{Synthetic benchmark.} Twelve artefact families at five severities over the
five base videos ($300$ clips): the four families of
Section~\ref{sec:problem:battery} (colour drift, chunk-boundary, flicker, identity
degradation), two progressive long-range families (identity drift, background drift),
and a six-transform flip control family (horizontal, transpose, periodic, elastic,
channel-shuffle, invert) designed for sub-metric attribution with predicted-blind and
predicted-caught cells.

\paragraph{Evaluated methods.} MGLD-VSR \cite{he_mgld_2024}, Upscale-A-Video
\cite{zhou_upscale_2024}, and FlashVSR \cite{zhuang_flashvsr_2025}
(Section~\ref{sec:found:vsr}).
